Makeillust — AI illustration SaaS

2025 · Lead product designer · 8 weeks

UX research UI design Design system

01 — The problem

New users needed 9+ steps and a tutorial before generating their first illustration. Most trials ended before that moment.

02 — What I did

Interviewed 12 users, mapped the first-run flow, then rebuilt the editor around a prompt-first canvas with smart defaults.

03 — The outcome

First illustration in 3 clicks. Trial-to-paid conversion rose 38% and support tickets about ‘getting started’ halved.
